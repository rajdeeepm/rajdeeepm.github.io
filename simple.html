Projects

  1. Accessibility Agents

    UMich Human-AI Lab · Sep 2025 — Present

    Systematically quantifying the modality gap in SOTA computer-use agents under accessibility constraints to map failure modes and prototype adaptive decision policies.

    • CUA Benchmark
    • LSTM-POMDP
    • F1–F8 Taxonomy
    • PPO + SFT
    • Reproducible Eval
    Full details
    • Investigating: SOTA computer-use agents under preregistered accessibility constraints (keyboard-only, screen-reader, high-zoom), quantifying failure modes via controlled interventions.
    • Building: capability-aware benchmark with success rate, latency, action/error counts, an F1–F8 failure taxonomy, Wilson CIs and Cliff's δ in a fully reproducible evaluation suite.
    • Prototyping: LSTM-based POMDP decision policy mapping capability-aware observations to reliable keyboard/screen-reader actions, trained via SFT then PPO with action masking.

  2. EC-TSS

    Independent Research · Oct 2025 — Present

    Self-supervised temporal fusion that cuts portrait-matting flicker by 25% FCD and 24% HFTE without ground-truth alpha — stabilizing video mattes while avoiding EMA oversmoothing.

    • 25% FCD ↓
    • 24% HFTE ↓
    • Self-supervised
    • Motion-compensated
    • Single GPU
    • VideoMatting108
    Full details
    • Developed: lightweight, backbone-agnostic temporal fusion head (shallow U-Net) refining a frozen per-frame MODNet-style backbone with motion-compensated priors and pixel-wise fusion weights.
    • Engineered: Farnebäck optical-flow pipeline warping the previous fused matte and deriving reliability cues (flow magnitude, occlusion, edge-consistency gating, high-frequency energy) plus a learned confidence map.
    • Designed: self-supervised objective from raw frames only — EMA teacher consistency with reliability masking, symmetric spatial anchoring, edge/gradient consistency, photometric reconstruction.
    • Demonstrated: FCD −25%, HFTE −24%, FCD burst ratio −17% versus per-frame inference on VideoMatting108, closing most of EMA's stability gap without oversmoothing.
